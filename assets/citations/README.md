# Publication citations

The `.bib` files are based on INSPIRE exports. Each filename matches the paper's arXiv identifier. They are served locally so the download links also work without JavaScript. The three-loop ABJM export was added on 15 November 2026; the cosmology entry includes the acceptance note from [arXiv v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08512v3).

When updating a publication, refresh its export from the record's `links.bibtex` URL and check the title, authors, publication details, and arXiv identifier against the website and CV. Do not automatically promote a preprint to a journal article without verifying the published record.
