Amplitudes & positive geometry

Chia-Kai Kuo

Postdoctoral ResearcherMax Planck Institute for Physics · Garching, Germany

I explore the geometric structures behind quantum field theory, with a focus on scattering amplitudes, ABJM theory, and correlation functions.

I received my Ph.D. from National Taiwan University, supervised by Song He and Yu-Tin Huang, studying loop amplitudes in ABJM theory and their positive geometries.

01 Research

Research directions

Scattering amplitudes, correlation functions, and the structures that connect them.

Positive geometry for scattering amplitudes

01 /

I study how amplitudes can be represented as canonical differential forms on positive geometries, with a particular focus on ABJM theory and its amplituhedron-like structures.

Loop amplitudes and integrands

02 /

My work develops all-loop and finite-loop constructions for ABJM amplitudes, including unitarity cuts, dimensional reduction, and explicit two-loop integrands.

Correlation functions and correlahedra

03 /

I am interested in the geometric organization of correlation-function integrands, chamber decompositions, leading singularities, and pure conformal integral bases.

Cosmological wavefunction coefficients

04 /

Recent work extends orthogonal-Grassmannian ideas toward supersymmetric cosmological wavefunction coefficients and their analytic structure.

Selected work

Cosmology 2026 · Preprint

Beyond Discontinuities: Cosmological WFCs and the Supersymmetric Orthogonal Grassmannian

Reconstructs three- and four-point supersymmetric wavefunction coefficients from energy discontinuities using an inversion formula and orthogonal-Grassmannian invariants.

Read paper

Correlation functions 2026 · JHEP

Leading singularities and chambers of Correlahedron

Organizes four-point correlators into chamber forms and local loop integrands, revealing a pure-integral basis through four loops, including an elliptic sector.

Read paper

Scattering amplitudes 2023 · JHEP

The ABJM Amplituhedron

Builds a geometric framework for planar ABJM loop amplitudes, with explicit one-loop results up to ten points and two-loop results up to eight points.
