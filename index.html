Amplitudes & positive geometry

Chia-Kai Kuo

Postdoctoral ResearcherMax Planck Institute for Physics · Garching, Germany

I study the geometric and analytic structures of quantum field theory, focusing on scattering amplitudes, correlation functions, and cosmological wavefunction coefficients. My aim is to understand how these observables can be constructed from geometry, symmetry, and their singularities.

I received my Ph.D. in theoretical physics from National Taiwan University in 2024 under the supervision of Song He and Yu-tin Huang. My doctoral research focused on loop amplitudes in ABJM theory and their description in terms of positive geometry.

01 Research

Research directions

Across these projects, I use geometry and singularities to find simpler representations of physical observables.

Positive geometry and scattering amplitudes

01 /

I investigate geometric descriptions of scattering amplitudes in which canonical differential forms encode loop integrands. In ABJM theory, a three-dimensional supersymmetric field theory, the amplituhedron provides a setting for studying how physical singularities and unitarity arise from geometry.

Loop amplitudes and infrared structure

02 /

I combine generalized unitarity and geometric decompositions to construct loop integrands and study their integrated amplitudes. My work on the BDS ansatz in ABJM uses scaffolding triangulations to organize the infrared structure of two-loop amplitudes for arbitrary even numbers of external particles.

Correlation functions and conformal integrals

03 /

I study the geometry and analytic structure of correlators in planar N=4 super Yang-Mills theory. This includes chamber decompositions of the correlahedron for four-point correlators and conformal integral bases for two-loop five-point calculations, connecting geometric organization with explicit analytic results.

Cosmological wavefunctions

04 /

My recent work explores how Grassmannian geometry and supersymmetry organize cosmological wavefunction coefficients. I study how their full energy dependence can be reconstructed from discontinuities, and how the resulting expressions connect to scattering amplitudes in the flat-space limit.

2026 · Preprint

Cosmological wavefunctions

Beyond Discontinuities: Cosmological WFCs and the Supersymmetric Orthogonal Grassmannian

We combine an inversion formula with supersymmetric Grassmannian representations to reconstruct tree-level wavefunction coefficients from energy discontinuities. Explicit three- and four-point results connect the construction to scattering amplitudes in the flat-space limit.

Read paper

2026 · JHEP

Correlator geometry

Leading singularities and chambers of Correlahedron

We decompose the geometry of four-point correlators into chambers and relate their forms to local loop integrands. At four loops, this organization accommodates an elliptic contribution and guides the choice of pure integral bases.

Read paper

2023 · JHEP

Scattering from geometry

The ABJM Amplituhedron

We construct a positive geometry for planar ABJM loop integrands and develop a chamber-based approach to its canonical forms. This yields explicit one-loop integrands through ten points and two-loop integrands through eight points.
